A CHIP-8 emulator is a program that simulates the behavior of the CHIP-8, an interpreted programming language used in simple 8-bit computers, allowing the execution of classic games and applications designed for the CHIP-8 platform. You can view the <a href="https://github.com/shariqali-dev/chip8-emulator" target="_blank">repository here</a>.

The CHIP-8 emulator is simpler than other game systems to emulate, featuring only 35 opcodes. Once completed, it allows you to play classic games like Pong, Tetris, Space Invaders and more. This simplicity made it the perfect first project for me to dive into emulation.
